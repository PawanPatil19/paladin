# Keep social presence private and connections mutual

Paladin will not show public people dots or expose individual live locations for discovery. **Presence** exists only inside an authenticated, invite-only **Group** during its **Activity**. Every **Participant** begins hidden, then explicitly chooses approximate or precise **Visibility**; coordinates expire when updates become stale.

The map uses human initials, a self halo, a visible approximate-location halo, freshness fading, and **Circle Check** status. The interface communicates uncertainty instead of making every marker look equally precise or current.

Friendship begins after shared activity through **Kaki Connection**. “Move together again” is double-blind: unilateral intent is not revealed, and a connection appears only after mutual intent. Structured activity invitations are preferred over unrestricted direct messaging.

This decision favors trust and relevant connection over maximum discovery. A future discoverable experience may expose temporary activity cards with broad area, time, activity, vibe, and count range, but not people, exact meeting points, codes, or live coordinates before approval.
